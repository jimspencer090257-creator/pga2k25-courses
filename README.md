# pga2k25-courses
Listing of PGA 2K25 match making courses played.

## Courses

### Augusta National Golf Club
[![Augusta National Golf Club](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/9a/Augusta_National_Golf_Club_logo.svg/320px-Augusta_National_Golf_Club_logo.svg.png)](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/9a/Augusta_National_Golf_Club_logo.svg/320px-Augusta_National_Golf_Club_logo.svg.png)

Home of The Masters Tournament. Augusta National is one of the most famous and exclusive golf clubs in the world, located in Augusta, Georgia. Known for its pristine conditions, iconic Amen Corner, and the famous azalea blooms each spring.

---

### TPC Sawgrass
[![TPC Sawgrass](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/46/TPC_Sawgrass_logo.svg/320px-TPC_Sawgrass_logo.svg.png)](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/46/TPC_Sawgrass_logo.svg/320px-TPC_Sawgrass_logo.svg.png)

Host of THE PLAYERS Championship in Ponte Vedra Beach, Florida. TPC Sawgrass is famous for its island-green 17th hole, one of the most photographed and challenging par-3s in professional golf.

---

### Pebble Beach Golf Links
[![Pebble Beach Golf Links](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8c/Pebble-beach-golf-links.jpg/320px-Pebble-beach-golf-links.jpg)](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8c/Pebble-beach-golf-links.jpg/320px-Pebble-beach-golf-links.jpg)

Located on the Monterey Peninsula in California, Pebble Beach Golf Links overlooks the Pacific Ocean. It has hosted multiple U.S. Opens and is consistently ranked among the top public golf courses in the world.

---

### St Andrews Links (Old Course)
[![St Andrews Links](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8f/St_Andrews_golf_links_logo.svg/320px-St_Andrews_golf_links_logo.svg.png)](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8f/St_Andrews_golf_links_logo.svg/320px-St_Andrews_golf_links_logo.svg.png)

Known as the "Home of Golf," the Old Course at St Andrews in Scotland is the oldest golf course in the world and has hosted The Open Championship more times than any other course. The iconic Swilcan Bridge and Hell Bunker are landmarks of this historic links.

---

### Torrey Pines Golf Course (South Course)
[![Torrey Pines](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/d3/Torrey_Pines_Golf_Course_%28South_Course%29.jpg/320px-Torrey_Pines_Golf_Course_%28South_Course%29.jpg)](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/d3/Torrey_Pines_Golf_Course_%28South_Course%29.jpg/320px-Torrey_Pines_Golf_Course_%28South_Course%29.jpg)

Situated on the cliffs overlooking the Pacific Ocean in La Jolla, California, Torrey Pines South Course hosted the 2021 U.S. Open. It is a public course operated by the City of San Diego and offers stunning ocean views throughout the round.
